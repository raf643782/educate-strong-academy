# Claims and Accreditation Scope Register

Internal reference for every public accreditation, endorsement, partnership, and personal-achievement claim on the Educate.Strong Academy website. No credentials or secret values are recorded here.

**Purpose:** prevent any future edit from broadening a confirmed claim beyond its exact confirmed scope. Every claim below was owner-confirmed as legitimate; nothing here is a request for further proof. The scope notes exist so future editors don't accidentally imply a relationship covers more than it does.

**Last updated:** Priority 2 (Master Continuation Programme) — 2026-07-23.
